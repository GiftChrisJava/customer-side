About Fusion Grill

Fusion Grill is Lilongwe's premier BBQ and grill restaurant, blending traditional African flavors with modern culinary techniques. Established in 2018, we've been serving the community with passion, using only the freshest locally-sourced ingredients. Our charcoal-grilled meats are marinated for 24 hours, ensuring maximum flavor in every bite.
