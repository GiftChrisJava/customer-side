The food was genuinely excellent — peri peri chicken was a hit with the whole family and the BBQ ribs were the best we've had in Lilongwe. Portions are generous for the price. Service let it down though: our waiter was friendly but slow — we had to ask twice for drink refills and the wait between courses was longer than it should have been for a weeknight. If they tighten up the service side, this place would be flawless.
